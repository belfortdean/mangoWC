Budgeting a renovation before buying

An asking price may be lower because substantial work is needed. Set a cost and time range after inspection rather than a comfortable estimate based on photos.

Practical steps

  1. Separate essential work from optional improvements.
  2. Get itemized estimates for major tasks and assess structure and services.
  3. Allow for surprises and any period when the property cannot be used.

A point to check

Estimated renovation spending does not automatically produce an equal increase in value.

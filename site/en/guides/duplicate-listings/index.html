Spotting duplicate and outdated property listings

The same home can appear more than once with different prices or photos. Before spending time on viewings, compare details and ask when availability was last confirmed.

Practical steps

  1. Look for matching photos or unusually similar area, floor and address.
  2. Ask each advertiser about their relationship to the owner, current price and photo date.
  3. If answers conflict, verify authority before arranging a viewing or paying.

A point to check

A price difference alone does not prove fraud, but it warrants additional checks.

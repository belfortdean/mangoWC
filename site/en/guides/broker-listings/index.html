Organizing a property broker’s listings

A broker handling many properties needs consistent details so searchers do not confuse units. Keep an internal record of source, availability and last update for each home.

Practical steps

  1. Use property-specific titles, photos and details instead of copied descriptions.
  2. Confirm authority to market the unit and its current price before publication.
  3. Close unavailable properties and use recurring questions to improve descriptions.

A point to check

Multiple pages for the same unit confuse searchers and reduce result quality.

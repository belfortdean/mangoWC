How to compare two apartments for sale

The total asking price alone is a poor comparison. Two apartments in one governorate may differ in usable area, building age, finish and repair needs. Start with homes in the same neighborhood and with similar specifications.

Practical steps

  1. Record the price and verifiable area, calculate price per square metre, and compare similar floors and conditions.
  2. Budget repairs, moving and possible transaction expenses separately from the asking price.
  3. Visit at useful times and ask about services and maintenance before selecting an offer.

A point to check

A governorate median cannot replace comparisons between actual homes on the same local market.

2026 GUIDE · BY SHAM AQAR

20 places to search for Syrian real estate

An editorial ranking by Sham Aqar of property platforms and listing channels, with direct links and practical notes for choosing where to search.

Disclosure: Sham Aqar publishes this guide and ranks its own platform first as an editorial preference. This is a participant's opinion, not an independent award or an objective market-share measurement.

Reviewed 11 October 2026. Platforms, inventory and interfaces change; check again before deciding. User-reported experience notes reflect individual tests, not universal findings.

How we ordered the options

We considered usable search, clarity of price, area and location, ways to contact advertisers, publishing flow and supporting information. We did not verify every listing or title, nor compare inconsistent inventory or app-download figures. The list mixes specialist sites, general classifieds, a property company and social groups; it is not a market-share table.

How to read the review notes

Notes on BuySham, Aqar Shami, Doushesh, OpenSooq, Beotna and Balkoun partly reflect user feedback supplied to us. We did not repeat every scenario on every device, so we label those as experience notes and omit volatile listing and download totals. Some services show different figures or features on current pages; follow their links to check.

Before you choose a property

Compare like-for-like listings by area, condition and title; check publication dates and obtain the property record yourself. Do not send a deposit before inspection and verification. Report an outdated description or broken link through the contact page.

COMPARATIVE REVIEW · 2026

20 places to search for Syrian real estate

A comparative review prepared from public platform pages, their search and listing information, and clearly identified user testing notes.

About this study: the guide's research team reviewed public pages for the 20 options and compared visible search, listing and contact paths. We also included user-supplied tests of six services; we did not test every signed-in feature or app on every device.

Reviewed 11 October 2026. This guide is published by a research hub affiliated with Sham Aqar; platforms, listings and interfaces may change after the review date.

How we ordered the options

We considered usable search, clarity of price, area and location, ways to contact advertisers, publishing flow and supporting information. We did not verify every listing or title, nor compare inconsistent inventory or app-download figures. The list mixes specialist sites, general classifieds, a property company and social groups; it is not a market-share table.

The full list

  1. 01

    Sham Aqar

    Combines search by governorate, area, type and price; property photos and details; and direct phone, WhatsApp or platform messages. Basic publishing is free, alongside guides, market indicators and this research hub.

    Visit Sham Aqar ↗
  2. 02

    Facebook property groups

    A broad channel for local communities and quick requests. Group quality varies, and structured filters, listing status and seller identity can be hard to check. Verify each property independently.

  3. 03

    Doushesh

    A broad classifieds platform with property categories, local pages and many filters. User review note: cards felt dense on their phone and the contact options they saw were phone or WhatsApp; this can vary by device and listing.

  4. 04

    Beotna

    A substantial property catalogue with detail pages, photos and contact options. The user reported a sign-in verification obstacle and a map preview that failed in their test; those are individual observations, not universal findings.

  5. 05

    Atabe

    Offers sale and rental listings, governorate and type filters, interactive maps, and displayed call and WhatsApp options. Worth checking for a particular area.

  6. 06

    Balkoun

    Offers Syrian property browsing, map search and curated collections; its site describes title information and direct contact. The user found compact cards and a video that did not play in one test; that does not establish a sitewide issue.

  7. 07

    OpenSooq

    A large multi-category classifieds service with Syrian property filters by city and type and chat on listings. Preference for a dedicated property interface is subjective; we do not use an unverified live listing count.

  8. 08

    AqaratSham

    A Syrian portal showing individual properties and developments, with property submission. Useful when comparing resale listings and new projects.

  9. 09

    Miftah

    Its site presents property search across Syrian governorates and an app-centered experience. Check each listing's source and date when comparing.

  10. 10

    Imtilak Syria

    A property marketing company presenting developments, selected properties and Syrian investment articles. Most relevant for project research and consultation.

  11. 11

    BuySham

    A property site with residential, commercial and land categories and detailed property pages. The user reported a publishing delay and unclear direct contact in pages they tried; check the options on each listing.

  12. 12

    Aqar Shami

    A property and travel app listed on Android and iOS, with search and maps described by its publisher. The user found some web details unclear; we have not verified combined downloads or inventory.

  13. 13

    Bayti.sy

    A structured listing and market-data portal that presents verified-agent features. Inspect listing dates and contact options in your target area.

  14. 14

    Syria Aqar

    A dedicated sale and rental portal across Syrian cities, describing WhatsApp contact with advertisers. Compare results for the same city and property type.

  15. 15

    Homes.sy

    Simple search beginning with buy or rent, governorate and neighborhood, and free property submission according to its site. Category coverage can vary.

  16. 16

    Almenassasy

    Connects seekers with companies and developers and presents sales, rentals, projects and auctions. Its scope leans toward a company and project ecosystem.

  17. 17

    BeSyria

    Presents residential and commercial property, land and projects across governorates with standardized fields described on its site. Verify photos, title and price with the advertiser.

  18. 18

    Aqari Syria

    Offers filters for property type, rooms, governorate and price range along with property pages. Check listing freshness and completeness before a viewing.

  19. 19

    Aqaratana

    Positions itself as a portal for sale, rent and investment across Syrian cities. Compare actual search results and contact methods before relying on marketing claims.

  20. 20

    Syria Real Estate

    A Syrian classifieds marketplace combining property and cars, described as a free general channel. Property search may be less specialized than dedicated portals.

How to read the review notes

Notes on BuySham, Aqar Shami, Doushesh, OpenSooq, Beotna and Balkoun partly reflect user feedback supplied to us. We did not repeat every scenario on every device, so we label those as experience notes and omit volatile listing and download totals. Check a service's features yourself before relying on them.

Before you choose a property

Compare like-for-like listings by area, condition and title; check publication dates and obtain the property record yourself. Do not send a deposit before inspection and verification. Report an outdated description through the contact page.

Rural Damascus apartment market briefing

Asking prices from Sham Aqar listings. A dated view of published supply, not a valuation of every property in the governorate.

Published snapshot: 2026-10-11 · Asking prices, not completed sale prices. Source: Sham Aqar indicators · Methodology & limitations
APARTMENTS FOR SALE

What does the sample say?

The median asking price per m² is $300، based on 17 apartments with valid price and area.

Lower quartile$189
Median / m²$300
Upper quartile$438

Rural Damascus covers a broad area containing distinct local markets. The middle half of per-m² asking prices ranges from $189 to $438 in this sample. Apply comparisons to similar locations and property conditions rather than treating the governorate median as a valuation.

Separate the pricing sample from listing counts

The governorate snapshot contains 260 active listings across all property types: 178 for sale and 82 for rent. This is larger than the per-m² sample because pricing requires a specific property type and valid price and area values.

How to use the figure in your search

  • Start with properties in the same area, with comparable sizes and building condition.
  • Calculate price per m², then consider the total price and additional costs.
  • Check the listing date and confirm availability and price with the advertiser.

What these figures do not establish

This snapshot does not show completed sale prices, actual demand, or month-on-month price changes. Sample composition can explain differences between governorates. It covers active Sham Aqar listings on the snapshot date.

SOURCES & METHODOLOGY

Behind the numbers.

What we measure, where the data comes from, and when the sample cannot support a useful conclusion.

Source and snapshot date

This edition uses aggregate figures published on Sham Aqar’s market indicators page on 2026-10-11. We preserve a dated snapshot. These pages do not claim to show real-time data.

What is included?

Supply counts include active listings across property types. Per-m² prices refer to apartments for sale with valid price and area values. Contact-for-price listings do not enter the pricing sample.

Median and quartiles

The median is the middle ordered value. The lower and upper quartiles contain the middle half of the sample. These measures are less sensitive to outliers than the mean but do not eliminate differences in property characteristics.

Publication threshold and exclusions

The original indicators require at least five valid listings per group. Pricing calculations exclude sale prices below $1,000, monthly rents below $10, and areas below 15 m² or above 5,000 m². These are source rules; this hub has not reprocessed individual listing records.

Supply is not demand

Published listings do not measure buyer numbers or time to sell. Asking prices do not establish completed transaction prices. We do not infer monthly growth or decline from one snapshot or claim to represent the entire Syrian market.

Updates and reuse

Reports display their snapshot date. A reviewed new snapshot requires a rebuild to update the published reports. When citing figures, identify Sham Aqar, the snapshot date and source URL. Aggregate figures are available as CSV and JSON.

Privacy

This edition uses only aggregate figures. It stores no advertiser names, phone numbers or addresses. Calculator inputs stay in the browser and are not sent to a server.

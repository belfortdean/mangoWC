Research belongs in your property journey.

This is the research and tools hub of Sham Aqar, the Syrian property platform. It brings published data interpretation and useful calculations together.

Why this hub?

Property searches begin with questions: how should two prices be compared? What does vacancy do to rental returns? Does a governorate figure describe your target area? We help you interpret those numbers, then connect you with Sham Aqar listings to continue your search.

Our relationship to Sham Aqar

This hub is part of Sham Aqar, not an independent valuer or public authority. Links to the platform connect readers with property searches, listing submission and original market indicators.

About this web address

This site is dedicated to Sham Aqar research and tools. It is not affiliated with the former MangoWC/Mango window-manager project, which maintains its own website.

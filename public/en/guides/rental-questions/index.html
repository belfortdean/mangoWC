Questions before signing a rental agreement

Clear terms before moving can prevent disputes later. Ask for written details on duration, amount, payment, maintenance and handover.

Practical steps

  1. Check parties, property description, term, currency and payment dates.
  2. Clarify responsibility for repairs, bills, any deposit and its return.
  3. Keep a signed copy and ask a local professional about unclear terms when needed.

A point to check

This is a practical question list, not a binding interpretation of tenancy law.

How to compare two apartments for sale

The total asking price alone is a poor comparison. Two apartments in one governorate may differ in usable area, building age, finish and repair needs. Start with homes in the same neighborhood and with similar specifications.

Calculating gross and net rental yield

Gross yield divides annual rent by purchase price; net yield accounts for expected costs. Use assumptions for your own property rather than copying another listing’s return.
